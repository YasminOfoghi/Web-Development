Hometown

Ballarat

Located in the Central Highlands, Victoria, Australia

Ballarat is where I was born and grew up. I left Ballarat when I was 5! It's about 105 km west of Melbourne, roughly an hour and a half by car or train, and it's known for its gold rush history, wide Victorian-era streetscapes, and the botanical gardens along Lake Wendouree.

It's a much quieter pace of life than the city, but it has a strong creative and student community, and it's close enough to Melbourne that I can get down for a day trip whenever I need to.

"Every second building in the CBD has a plaque on it. You can't walk two blocks without tripping over 170 years of history".
